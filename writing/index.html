Writing

Welcome to my personal writing archive of ideas, questions, and reflections that I choose to share publicly. I write about science and emerging technologies, research and higher education, science policy, and the role of universities in society. I also write about learning, leadership, intellectual curiosity, and the experiences that shape how we think and work.

These pieces are not intended to provide definitive answers. They are part of an ongoing effort to understand complex questions, examine assumptions, and make sense of a rapidly changing world.
