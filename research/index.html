Research Overview

Exploring quantum materials, sustainable energy systems, and the wider implications of science and emerging technologies

My research focuses on quantum materials and functional materials for sustainable energy applications. I investigate the physical mechanisms underlying emergent phenomena by designing, synthesizing, and experimentally characterizing novel materials. My work draws on advanced techniques such as X-ray diffraction, magnetometry, spectroscopy, and muon spin spectroscopy, supported increasingly by computational and data-driven approaches.

Alongside my laboratory research, I am interested in the broader relationship between science, technology, education, and society. This includes the public understanding of science, the governance of emerging technologies, and the role of higher education in developing scientific capability and human capacity.

Research Areas

⚛️

Quantum & Emerging Technologies

Exploring quantum and emerging technologies to design and optimize next-generation materials that enable a sustainable technological future.

🔬

Advanced Materials

Designing functional materials, with advanced characterization expertise in muon spin spectroscopy at TRIUMF, PSI, and RAL, to investigate their behavior under extreme conditions, including high pressure, ultra-high magnetic fields, and sub-millikelvin temperatures.

🏛️

Science, Technology & Governance

Studying how scientific breakthroughs and emerging technologies reshape societies by influencing governance systems, economic structures, ethical discourse, and global power dynamics.
